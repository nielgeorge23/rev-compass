RevCompass — how the machine actually runs

One structure, three engines. This walkthrough shows the agents and data behind RevCompass, then traces three real scenarios — a payer drift, an appeal, and a contract variance — through the exact same pipeline.

RevCompass doesn't replace Epic or any other system of record — it sits above them. It reads what those systems already produce, normalizes it into one record type, and routes that record through seven purpose-built agents before anything reaches a person.
